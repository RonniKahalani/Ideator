# AGENT.md: instructions for AI-Agents

These instructions add to Ideator's built-in rules. They never override
privacy, never-send, no-delete or the untouched template.

## Business context
- We are a 6-person service design studio in Copenhagen.
- Clients are mostly municipalities, housing associations and cultural venues.
- Assume a public-sector client unless the problem says otherwise.

## Voice and language
- Plain English, short sentences, no buzzwords ("synergy", "leverage", "disrupt").
- Write for a client project manager, not a designer.

## Sources
- Prefer English public data and peer-reviewed studies.
- Use news only for recent local context. Never use vendor marketing pages.
- At least 2 of the sources must be English.

## Constraints every idea must respect
- No ideas that need new hardware over £2500.
- Must work for users with no smartphone.
- GDPR: no ideas that collect personal data beyond a name and contact.

## Scoring
- Add a criterion "Cost to pilot" (1-5, 5 = cheapest).
- Weight "Accessibility" double.

## Pretotypes
- Prefer paper prototypes, signage tests, Wizard-of-Oz and landing pages.
- Never suggest paid ads or anything that contacts real people without consent.

## Report extras
- On the Deliver tab, add a "Risks" note per concept (1-2 lines).
- End with 3 next steps a client could start within 2 weeks.

## Don't
- Don't recommend specific commercial vendors by name.
- Don't propose ideas that need new staff hires.
