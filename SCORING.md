# Scoring

This is how challenges and ideas are scored.

## Challenge scoring

Finding the most **exciting** and **possible** challenges.

* X axis \= from **boring** to **exciting**.  
* Y axis \= from **impossible** to **possible**.

## Idea scoring

Finding the most **simple** and **cheap** ideas.

* X axis \= from **complex** to **simple**.  
* Y axis \= from **expensive** to **cheap**.
