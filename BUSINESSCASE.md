# A Business case
Imagine, you own a small design studio startup and you wants to solve real problems for your customers.

## Mission
Research the challenges behind problems our customers struggle with, generate lots of ideas and concepts to explore new business opportunities or solutions, in specific markets.

## Resources
You'd need a team of people for research, design, finance, production, HR, facilitation.... and a lot of time and money to deliver a proper and productive team to solve these problems the best way possible.

## Data is key
Ideas and opinions (assumptions) don't guarantee anything, you need to tests your assumptions, in real life, and gather YODA (**YO**ur **DA**ta).

## Build - Measure - Learn
You must continuously verify: Are we [Building the Right It](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3sUozPcH4fY&t=4s&pp=ygUSYnVpbGQgdGhlIHJpZ2h0IGl0)?

## How Ideator can help you
Here are some of the benefits of using Ideator:
### Reduces waste of time and money
- Project time, effort and cost drops from weeks to minutes.

### Automated realtime ideation
- Research, ideation, decisionmaking working for you 24/7, solving problems.

### Faster client intake
- Paste the client's problem in plain words and get a structured Double Diamond report back in about 7 to 10 minutes.

### Sharper scoping
- The discover and define steps break a messy brief into clear sub-problems before anyone commits hours.
- The laser-cutter run split one complaint into three user groups, each with its own pain point.

### Proposal research with sources
- Every report carries sourced findings, five by default, so a pitch rests on real references instead of guesses.

### Local context built in
- It works out the country from the problem, so the research fits the client's market.

### A scored idea shortlist
- Ideas and concepts are scored side by side, which gives you a defensible "why this direction" for client conversations.

### Ready-made workshop material
- Pretotypes, which are cheap fake-it tests, plus drafted materials give you a starting kit for a client workshop or a quick validation sprint.

### A consistent deliverable
- Every run lands in the same studio template as a Google Sheet, so reports look alike across clients and are easy to compare.

### Private by default
- Each report is a private sheet in your own Google Drive, never shared, and nothing gets sent or posted on its own.
- Client work stays confidential until you choose to share it.

### Adjustable per job
- You can turn the number of ideas, pretotypes and research sources up or down for a quick scope check or a deeper proposal.

### One maintained workflow
- It pulls its workflow files from the kit folder on GitHub before every run, so improving the studio's process once, updates every future report.

### Internal process audits
- Point it at your own studio problems, like overrunning projects or handoff gaps between designers.
- The S-train bike-parking and makerspace runs show it handles everyday operational problems like these, not just product ideas.

### Less blank-page time
You're not starting from scratch anymore:
- Juniors get a structured first pass to critique and build on.
- Seniors spend their time on judgment instead of setup.
